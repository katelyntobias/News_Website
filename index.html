ARCHERS NETWORK

EST. 202X · DE LA SALLE UNIVERSITY

Stories that move the community.

Archers Network is a student-led media organization dedicated to telling the stories, capturing the moments, and amplifying the voices that shape the Lasallian community.

OUR MISSION

Stories that matter.

Archers Network is dedicated to telling meaningful stories that reflect the experiences, achievements, and voices of the Lasallian community.

Through journalism, photography, and multimedia, we aim to inform, connect, and give the community a space to be heard.

MEET THE TEAM

The people behind the stories.

Archers Network is made up of student writers, photographers, creatives, and storytellers who work together to document the Lasallian community.
